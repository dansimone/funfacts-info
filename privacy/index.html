Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary: One Fun Fact does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no network connections, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing ever leaves your device.

What Data the App Collects

None. One Fun Fact does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal information whatsoever.

No Network, No Accounts

All facts are bundled inside the app and displayed entirely on your device. The app makes no network connections and has no sign-in or accounts.

On-Device Storage

The app remembers one small preference on your device — which fact you're on and whether you're viewing short or all facts — so it can pick up where you left off. This never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

No Analytics or Tracking

One Fun Fact contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services. We do not collect usage data or behavioral information.

Children's Privacy

One Fun Fact is safe for children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It complies with children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K) by simply not collecting, using, or disclosing any personal information.

Changes to This Policy
