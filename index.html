One Fun Fact

Tap. Fun fact. Repeat.

The simplest fun-facts app there is. It shows you one fun fact. Tap the screen for a different one. That's it — no menus, no accounts, no ads.

Press and hold to switch between short facts and all of them, and add the widget to your Home Screen. Every fact is checked for accuracy and family-friendliness, and works completely offline.
